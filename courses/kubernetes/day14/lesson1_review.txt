# Lesson: Kubernetes Mastery Review — Before the Capstone

## [INTENT] "Review the complete K8s operations lifecycle."

## The Kubernetes resource hierarchy:

  Cluster
  ├── Nodes (machines)
  │   └── Pods (containers)
  │       └── Containers (your apps)
  │
  ├── Deployments (manage pods, replicas, rolling updates)
  ├── StatefulSets (manage stateful pods with stable identity)
  │
  ├── Services (stable networking)
  │   ├── ClusterIP (internal)
  │   ├── NodePort (node access)
  │   └── LoadBalancer (external)
  │
  ├── Ingress (HTTP routing, TLS)
  │
  ├── ConfigMaps (non-sensitive config)
  ├── Secrets (sensitive config)
  │
  ├── PersistentVolumeClaims (storage requests)
  │
  ├── HPA (auto-scaling)
  ├── PDB (disruption budgets)
  ├── NetworkPolicy (security)
  │
  └── Helm Charts (packaging)

## Debugging order:
  1. kubectl get pods — what's the status?
  2. kubectl describe pod — what went wrong?
  3. kubectl logs — what did the app say?
  4. kubectl exec — get inside and look around
  5. kubectl get events — cluster-level happenings

## Operational readiness checklist:
  Every pod: resource limits, health probes
  Every service: correct selectors, verified endpoints
  Every deployment: multiple replicas, rolling update strategy
  Every secret: encrypted, access-controlled
  The cluster: monitored, backed up, upgrade-planned
